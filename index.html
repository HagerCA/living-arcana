Prepared by SAGE for Christopher Hager · October 2026
I

The Ladder

Every archetype in this deck can be lived at five depths. The archetype says what someone carries. The ladder says how well they carry it.

Three questions place anyone Which archetype leads? Under pressure, which shadow pole do they swing to? Is the mature form a state they visit, or a trait they live?

The King, Warrior, Magician and Lover, with their boy forms and twin shadows, come from Robert Moore and Douglas Gillette's King, Warrior, Magician, Lover (1990). Every other archetype here is SAGE's extension of their structure.
